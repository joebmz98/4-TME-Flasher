How it works
  • Changes reach 4-TME as you turn a knob, and 4-TME's screen shows the value just like turning its encoder. Turning an encoder on 4-TME moves the knob here.
  • Knobs: drag up and down, scroll, or use the arrow keys (Shift for fine steps when dragging, PgUp/PgDn for big ones). Double-click puts a knob back to the INIT value.
  • Click a voice to edit it (the panel's voice buttons). Shift works like holding SHIFT: click voices to choose a group, and each knob moves every voice in the group by the same amount from where it is.
  • Mute works like BACK + voice button: the voice stays in the round-robin but is silent.
  • 4-TME keeps the sound it is playing through power-off by itself, two seconds after the last change. Presets are only written when you save one.
  • Presets live in 4-TME's SPI flash, so firmware updates keep them. A backup file holds every used slot with its name and all 64 values; restoring writes those slots back.
